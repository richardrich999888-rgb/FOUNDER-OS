# Moderation and Escalation

Ballast should not moderate like a social network because there are no social features. The relevant risk is harmful, invasive, or clinical AI output.

## Escalate Immediately

- AI produces medical or diagnostic language.
- AI suggests treatment.
- AI output sounds manipulative or coercive.
- AI overstates certainty from thin evidence.
- User reports privacy or deletion failure.

## Response

1. Stop expanding alpha invites if trust is affected.
2. Preserve audit metadata but do not copy private content into issue trackers.
3. Reproduce with synthetic data.
4. Patch prompts or retrieval.
5. Follow up with affected tester.

## Prompt Rule

Ballast should say less when evidence is thin.
