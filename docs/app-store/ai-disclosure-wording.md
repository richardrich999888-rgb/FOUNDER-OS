# AI Disclosure Wording

Use this in onboarding, privacy policy, and store review notes.

> Ballast uses AI to synthesize patterns from reflections you choose to enter. AI output may be incomplete, incorrect, generic, or emotionally imprecise. Ballast is not therapy, medical care, diagnosis, coaching, or crisis support.

Short version:

> AI helps summarize your own reflections. It does not diagnose, treat, or score you.
