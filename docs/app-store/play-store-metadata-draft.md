# Play Store Metadata Draft

## App Name

Ballast

## Short Description

Cognitive observability for founders.

## Full Description

Ballast is a private reflection system for founders and ambitious builders.

Capture short reflections, search your own history, and review weekly patterns from your own entries. Ballast is designed for calm self-observation and repeated reflection.

Ballast is not therapy, medical care, diagnosis, coaching, or crisis support.

## Data Safety Notes

Ballast may collect account information, user-provided reflection content, app activity, diagnostics, and optional audio if voice reflection is enabled. Reflection content is used to provide app functionality and AI synthesis.

Do not claim data is collected for advertising or sale.
