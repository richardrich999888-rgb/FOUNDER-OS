Ballast is a private reflection system for founders and ambitious builders.

Use it to capture short reflections, search your own history, and review weekly patterns from your own entries. Ballast is designed to feel calm, restrained, and evidence-based.

Ballast is not therapy, medical care, diagnosis, coaching, or crisis support.
