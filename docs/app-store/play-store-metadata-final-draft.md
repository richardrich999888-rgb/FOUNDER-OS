# Play Store Metadata Final Draft

## App Title

Ballast

## Short Description

Cognitive observability for private reflection.

## Long Description

Ballast is a private reflection system for founders, operators, and ambitious builders.

Capture short reflections, search your own history, and review weekly patterns from your own entries. Ballast is designed for calm cognitive observability: noticing how you think, decide, recover, and avoid over time.

Ballast uses AI to help summarize patterns from reflections you choose to enter. AI output may be incomplete, generic, or wrong, so it should be treated as a reflective aid rather than advice.

Ballast is not therapy, medical care, diagnosis, coaching, or crisis support.

## Privacy Wording

Your reflections are private to your account. Ballast uses encrypted transport, secure authentication, and encrypted storage fields for sensitive reflection content. Reflection text is not sent to analytics.

## AI Disclosure

AI helps summarize your own reflections. It does not diagnose, treat, or score you.

## Onboarding Disclosure

Ballast is a private cognitive mirror. It is not therapy or medical care. Use it to notice patterns from your own reflections.
