# Founder Interview Script

Use this after a tester has used Ballast for at least one week.

## Opening

Ballast is not trying to be therapy or a productivity tracker. We are testing whether cognitive observability helps founders notice useful patterns and return voluntarily.

## Questions

1. What did Ballast understand correctly?
2. What felt generic?
3. What felt invasive?
4. Did search your own mind retrieve anything meaningful?
5. Did the weekly insight feel earned by your actual reflections?
6. Did you voluntarily return without being reminded?
7. Was anything emotionally uncomfortable in the wrong way?
8. Was the privacy explanation clear?
9. Would you miss Ballast if it disappeared?
10. Who else, if anyone, would you naturally recommend this to?

## Listen For

Strong signal:

- "It noticed something I had not connected."
- "I came back because I wanted to check a pattern."
- "It felt calmer than journaling."

Weak signal:

- "Cool idea."
- "Interesting."
- "I might use it if it had more features."

Risk signal:

- "It felt like generic AI."
- "It felt too therapeutic."
- "I was not sure where my data went."
