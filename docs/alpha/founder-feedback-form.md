# Founder Feedback Form

Use this as a Google Form, Tally form, Typeform, or manual interview sheet.

## Questions

1. What role best describes you?
2. How many reflections did you add?
3. Did you use search your own mind?
4. Did search retrieve something meaningful?
5. Did the weekly insight notice something useful?
6. Did anything feel generic?
7. Did anything feel invasive?
8. Did any wording feel too therapeutic or clinical?
9. Did you voluntarily return?
10. Would you miss Ballast if it disappeared?
11. What one thing would make you more likely to use it next week?
