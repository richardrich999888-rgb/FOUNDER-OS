# Alpha Recruitment Flow

Goal: recruit testers who can give useful evidence about retention, trust, and cognitive observability.

## Target Mix

- 10 founders
- 20 operators
- 15 creators

Invite in waves. Do not invite everyone at once.

## Qualification Questions

1. Are you actively building, operating, or creating something demanding?
2. Are you willing to add at least three reflections in one week?
3. Are you comfortable giving blunt feedback?
4. Are you evaluating Ballast as a private reflection system, not therapy?

## Invite Stages

1. Warm DM or email.
2. Consent language.
3. TestFlight or closed Play link.
4. One-week use period.
5. Founder interview.
6. Retention review.

## Rejection Criteria

Do not invite someone if they are seeking clinical support, crisis support, diagnosis, or treatment.
