# Alpha Tester Instructions

Thank you for testing Ballast.

Ballast is a private cognitive mirror. It is meant to help you observe patterns in your own reflections. It is not therapy, medical care, diagnosis, coaching, or a productivity score.

## What To Do This Week

1. Add at least three reflections.
2. Keep reflections short if that feels easier.
3. Search for a pattern you are curious about.
4. Generate a weekly insight.
5. Rate whether the insight felt useful.

## What We Are Testing

- Did Ballast notice something specific?
- Did the app feel trustworthy?
- Did any wording feel too clinical or invasive?
- Did search your own mind retrieve something meaningful?
- Did you return voluntarily?

## What Not To Enter

Do not enter emergency, medical, legal, or highly sensitive information during alpha.

## How To Give Feedback

Use the in-app ratings first. Then send direct feedback after one week:

- what felt useful
- what felt generic
- what felt invasive
- what would make you return
- whether you would miss the app if removed
