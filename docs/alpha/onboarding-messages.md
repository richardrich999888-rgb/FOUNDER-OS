# Alpha Onboarding Messages

## Invite DM

I am opening a small private alpha for Ballast.

It is cognitive observability for founders: a private way to notice patterns in how you think, decide, recover, and avoid. It is not therapy, coaching, or a productivity tracker.

I am looking for blunt feedback on whether it notices anything useful enough that you would voluntarily return.

## TestFlight Email

Subject: Ballast private alpha invite

Thanks for testing Ballast.

This alpha asks for one simple thing: add three honest reflections this week, try search your own mind, then generate a weekly insight.

What matters:

- did it feel specific?
- did it feel trustworthy?
- did it feel generic?
- did you come back voluntarily?

Please avoid entering emergency, medical, legal, or highly sensitive information during alpha.

## Day 3 Check-In

Quick check: have you added at least one reflection yet?

No pressure to write a lot. Short, real signals are more useful than polished journaling.

## Day 7 Interview Ask

Can I ask you 20 minutes of blunt questions about Ballast?

The main thing I want to understand is whether it noticed something meaningful or felt like generic AI.
