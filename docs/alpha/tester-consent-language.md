# Tester Consent Language

Use before inviting a tester.

> Ballast is in private alpha. It is a reflection and cognitive observability tool, not therapy, medical care, coaching, diagnosis, or crisis support. You should not enter emergency, medical, legal, or highly sensitive information during alpha.
>
> The product uses AI to synthesize patterns from reflections you choose to enter. AI output can be incomplete, wrong, generic, or emotionally off. Please treat it as a reflective aid, not advice.
>
> We collect privacy-safe usage events such as reflection creation, search usage, and usefulness ratings. We do not send raw reflection text, transcripts, prompts, AI outputs, or raw search queries to analytics.
>
> You can request export or deletion from settings.
