# Feedback Collection Workflows

Feedback exists to improve trust, retrieval, and repeated use.

## In-App Feedback

Collect:

- reflection usefulness
- retrieval success
- weekly insight usefulness
- AI output quality tags

Do not collect:

- raw reflection text in analytics
- raw search query in analytics
- AI prompt text
- transcript text

## Twice-Weekly Review

Tuesday:

- review failed retrieval ratings
- review weekly insight ratings
- identify generic AI patterns

Friday:

- choose one retrieval improvement
- choose one synthesis prompt improvement
- choose one friction reduction

## Feedback Labels

Use these labels in notes:

- `generic-ai`
- `invasive-tone`
- `retrieval-miss`
- `reflection-fatigue`
- `trust-issue`
- `useful-pattern`
- `voluntary-return`

## Escalation

Escalate immediately if a tester says:

- they cannot delete data
- the app feels like therapy
- the app made a medical or diagnostic claim
- AI output felt manipulative
- private content appeared in logs, screenshots, or analytics
