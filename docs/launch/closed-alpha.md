# Stage 2: Closed Alpha

Goal: test emotional resonance and repeated use with 10-25 trusted users.

Do not invite random users. The alpha should be founders, operators, creators, and emotionally articulate high-performers.

## Recruiting Criteria

Good alpha users:

- currently building or operating something difficult
- willing to reflect honestly
- comfortable giving blunt feedback
- likely to use voice or short daily notes
- sensitive to whether AI feels generic

Avoid:

- users seeking therapy
- users looking for a habit tracker
- users who only want productivity dashboards
- users who will not give feedback

## Feedback Questions

Ask after the first session:

- Did the app notice something useful?
- Did it feel generic?
- Did it feel invasive?
- Did it feel emotionally intelligent?
- Was any wording uncomfortable or too clinical?

Ask after one week:

- Did you voluntarily return?
- Did weekly insight feel earned by your reflections?
- Did search your own mind retrieve something meaningful?
- Would you miss Ballast if it disappeared?

## Alpha Operating Rhythm

- Week 1: 10 users, founder-led onboarding calls.
- Week 2: fix obvious trust and retrieval issues.
- Week 3: add 10-15 more users only if Week 1 feedback is credible.
- Week 4: decide whether to proceed to soft launch or continue alpha.
