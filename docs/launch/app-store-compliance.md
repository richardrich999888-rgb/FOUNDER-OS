# App Store and Play Store Compliance

Ballast is trust-sensitive and emotionally sensitive. Store copy must avoid medical, therapeutic, diagnostic, or treatment claims.

This checklist is based on current Apple and Google guidance as of May 15, 2026. Re-check the official guidelines before submission.

## iOS Requirements

- Apple Developer Account.
- App Privacy labels in App Store Connect.
- Privacy Policy URL.
- Terms of Service URL.
- In-app account deletion.
- Sign in with Apple if Google login is offered.
- Support URL.
- Contact email.
- App screenshots.
- App review notes and demo account if needed.
- Accurate microphone permission copy if voice reflection is enabled.

## Android Requirements

- Google Play Console account.
- Data Safety form.
- Privacy Policy URL.
- In-app account deletion.
- Web deletion URL for Play Console.
- App content declarations.
- Accurate microphone permission declaration if voice reflection is enabled.

## Store Copy Rules

Allowed:

- "Reflect on patterns in your work and life."
- "Search your own reflections."
- "Build awareness through private cognitive observability."
- "Review weekly themes from your own notes."

Avoid:

- "Detect burnout."
- "Treat anxiety."
- "AI therapist."
- "Mental health diagnosis."
- "Depression prediction."

## Account Deletion Requirements

The app must provide an in-app path to start account deletion. The deletion path should be easy to find from settings.

Deletion should cover:

- account profile
- reflections
- AI memories
- weekly insights
- exports
- notification preferences
- voice files

Some operational records may need short retention for fraud, billing, tax, or security reasons. If retained, the privacy policy must say what is retained and why.
