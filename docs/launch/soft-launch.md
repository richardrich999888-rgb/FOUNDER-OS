# Stage 5: Soft Launch

Goal: 50-200 real users.

Do not run a broad public launch until retention signals are credible.

## Best Channels

- Founder Twitter/X.
- Founder communities.
- Indie hacker groups.
- Startup Discords.
- YC and operator circles.
- Warm introductions.

## Do Not Do Yet

- paid ads
- Product Hunt
- broad-market launch
- viral stunts
- mass creator campaigns

## Soft Launch Message

Position Ballast as cognitive observability:

> I am testing Ballast with a small group of founders. It is a private reflection system that helps you notice patterns in how you think, decide, and recover. It is not therapy or a productivity tracker. The question is simple: does it help you understand yourself clearly enough to come back?

## Invite Criteria

Invite someone only if:

- they understand the product is early
- they will use it for at least one week
- they will give honest feedback
- they are comfortable with private reflection

## Soft Launch Exit Criteria

- Week 1 retention is meaningfully above curiosity usage.
- Some users voluntarily use retrieval.
- Weekly insights receive specific positive feedback.
- At least a few users would miss the product if removed.
